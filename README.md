# Wrinkle Jump — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 22, 2026

Wrinkle Jump ("the game") is made by Dakota Unrau ("I", "me"). This policy explains what the game collects, why, and how you can delete it. The short version: the game only collects what it needs to run the global leaderboard, keep your koban coins and items safe, and deliver coin packs you buy. There are no ads, no tracking and no selling of data.

## What the game collects

When you finish a run, the game sends the following to its leaderboard server:

- **A random account ID.** The first time you play online, the game creates an anonymous account for your device. It isn't linked to your name, email, Apple ID or phone number unless you choose Sign in with Apple (below).
- **The player name you choose.** It's shown publicly on the leaderboard, so please don't use your real name.
- **Your score and run details:** score, time played, rows climbed, Treat Toobz collected and zoomies used. These are used to rank you and to catch cheating.
- **Your cat's appearance:** the colours, pattern and shape you pick in the Cat Studio, and the clothes it's wearing, so your cat can appear next to your score.
- **Your coins and items:** how many koban coins you've collected and spent, and which shop items you own, so they're kept safe and follow you between runs.
- **Coin pack purchases:** if you buy koban with real money, the game receives Apple's transaction ID, which pack you bought, and when. Payment is handled entirely by Apple: the game never sees your card or payment details. The transaction is checked with Apple so the coins are added exactly once, and refunded purchases are taken back.
- **Sign in with Apple (optional):** if you tap **Sign in with Apple** in Settings, your account is linked to your Apple ID, so your koban, items and best score can be restored on a new phone. The game then receives a private Apple account identifier and an email address from Apple. You can choose Apple's "Hide My Email", which gives a private relay address instead of your real one. The email is never shown publicly, and the game never emails you.
- **The game version and your device type** (for example "iOS"), to help fix problems.

Like any internet service, the server provider also receives your device's IP address when the game connects. It's used only to deliver the service and protect it from abuse.

## What the game does not collect

Unless you choose Sign in with Apple, the game does **not** collect any email address. It never collects your real name, contacts, photos, location, advertising identifiers or browsing history. It shows no ads, uses no analytics or tracking tools, and never sells or shares your data for advertising.

## How the data is used

The data is used only to:

- show the global leaderboard, including your name, score, rank and cat;
- keep the leaderboard fair by checking scores and blocking cheating and offensive names;
- keep your koban, items and coin pack purchases safe, and restore them if you signed in with Apple;
- keep the service running and fix problems.

## Where it is stored

Leaderboard data is stored with **Supabase** (supabase.com), a cloud database provider that hosts the leaderboard. Your settings, best score and cat design are also saved on your own device.

## Public information

Your **player name, score, rank and cat's appearance (including its outfit)** are visible to everyone on the leaderboard. Offensive names are filtered, and entries may be removed if they break the rules.

## Keeping and deleting your data

Your leaderboard data is kept until you delete it.

- **Delete my data:** in the game, open **Settings** (the gear button) and tap **Delete my data** twice. This permanently deletes your online account, your leaderboard entry, your submission history, your koban coins and your shop items (including anything bought with real money), and unlinks your Apple ID. A record of each real-money purchase (Apple's transaction ID, the pack and the date, no longer linked to you) is kept, because Apple can still report refunds and it's needed for accounting.
- Deleting the app removes the data saved on your device, but not your leaderboard entry. Use "Delete my data" first, or email us your player name and we'll delete it.

## Children

The game is suitable for all ages and doesn't ask for any personal information. Please choose a player name that doesn't reveal who you are.
